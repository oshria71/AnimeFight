Delete your account

You can delete your Anime Fight account and all its data in two ways:

In the app

Open Anime Fight, tap the account button at the top right, then Delete my account and Delete forever.

By email

Email oshria71@gmail.com from the Google address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your account, saved progress (coins, unlocks, gauntlet record), arena player, team, rating and match history. Nothing is kept. Guests have no account: their progress is only on their phone and goes when the app is uninstalled.
