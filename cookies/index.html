Cookies & storage

Last updated October 31, 2026

1. No tracking cookies

Anime Fight does not use advertising or analytics cookies, tracking pixels or third-party trackers. So there's no cookie banner: there is nothing to opt out of.

2. What the Game saves on your device

The Game uses your device's local storage, which works like a cookie but never leaves your device on its own:

  • Game progress: coins, unlocks, arena anime and gauntlet record.
  • Sound setting: whether sound is on.
  • Sign-in session: keeps you signed in, if you signed in with Google.
  • Sync state: which account this device last saved to, so progress isn't mixed up.

These are needed for the Game to work. Clearing the app's or site's data removes them (and a guest's progress). Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in page, under Google's policies.
