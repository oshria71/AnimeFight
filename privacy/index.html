Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

1. In short

  • Guests: your progress stays on your device. We don't receive it.
  • Signed in: we store your Google name, email, picture link, your progress and your arena play.
  • Your fighter name, team, rating and record are public in the rankings.
  • The phone app shows optional ads from Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one for a reward.
  • No analytics and no selling of data.
  • Delete your account any time from the Account screen; everything goes with it.

2. Who we are

Anime Fight is made by Oshri (independent developer of Anime Fight), based in Israel, who is the controller of your personal data. Contact: oshria71@gmail.com.

3. What we collect and why

When you play as a guest

Coins, unlocked seasons, arena anime, gauntlet record and your sound setting are saved only in your device's storage. Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Google tells us your account ID, name, email address and profile picture link. We use them to create your account, show who you're signed in as, and keep your progress. We never see your Google password. We save your progress (coins, unlocks, arena anime, gauntlet record) so it follows you to any device.

When you play the online arena

We store your fighter name, your team, rating, wins and losses, a log of your ranked matches (opponent, result, points and the fight's random seed), daily match counts, extra matches bought and weekly prizes. We use these to run matches, rankings and prizes and to check for cheating.

Technical data

Like any online service, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request information when the Game talks to its servers. Character pictures are loaded from AniList, which also receives your IP address. We don't use this to identify or track you.

When you email us

We use your email and message only to answer you.

4. What others can see

The rankings are public: anyone using the Game can see your fighter name, team, rating and win/loss record. Your email, Google name and picture are never shown to other players. Choose a fighter name that doesn't reveal who you are.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your data. We only use these service providers:

  • Supabase: hosts our database and sign-in, and stores the data above on our behalf.
  • Google: provides "Sign in with Google" under Google's own privacy policy.
  • AniList: serves character pictures (receives your IP address only).
  • Google AdMob (phone app only): loads and shows the optional reward ads. Google receives your IP address, your device's advertising ID and basic device information, under Google's own privacy policy. In the EU and UK the app asks for your choice first, and ads are only personalized if you agree; you can change it any time under Account → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset the advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect players or the Game from fraud or abuse, or to a new owner if the Game is transferred (who must keep this policy's promises).

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account or ask us to. Weekly ratings reset every Monday; your match log is kept while your account exists. Emails are kept as long as needed to deal with your request.

7. Deleting your account and data

Signed in, open Account → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your sign-in, your saved progress, your arena player, matches and prizes. Guest progress can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can also ask us by email at oshria71@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, sign-in uses Google with no passwords stored by us, and database rules let each player change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you as the law requires.

9. Children

The Game is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email oshria71@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Israel's Privacy Protection Law, the EU/UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access your data, get a copy of it, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Email oshria71@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Legal bases (EU/UK): running the Game you asked for (contract), keeping the Game fair and secure (legitimate interests), and meeting legal obligations. International transfers: our providers may store data outside your country; they use recognised safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority).

US residents: we collect identifiers (name, email, account ID, IP address) and game activity for the purposes above. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the Game about important changes.
